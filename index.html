Cards

There are two types of cards: Explore cards and Support cards. Click a card to flip it and see the question on the front and the explanation on the back.

Explore Cards

Explore cards give you a ready-made question to ask right after someone shares. These follow-up questions make it easy to stay curious and turn a quick share into a fuller story.

Support Cards

Support cards also invite you to reflect on your own experience. Each card gives you a sentence starter to complete, so your response feels specific and genuine — not just polite.
